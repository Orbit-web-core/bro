# LUMI DESIGN CONSTITUTION

This document serves as the hard-coded aesthetic source of truth. Any future modifications MUST adhere to these rules.

## 1. The Silhouette (The Pill)
- **Shape**: Lumi MUST always be a vertical "Pill" shape (rect with 100% border-radius on top/bottom).
- **Material**: "Claymorphic" Plastic. High-gloss white/off-white body with deep inner shadows and a "Subsurface Scattering" glow at the base.
- **Floating**: Lumi is never grounded. He must always have a `hover` animation (y-axis translation) to feel weightless and digital.

## 2. The Visor (The Soul)
- **Faceplate**: A dark, semi-transparent glass visor (indigo/slate-900).
- **Digital Eyes**: Two glowing neon pill-shaped eyes. They do not have pupils; they represent "LCD Matrix" elements.
- **Emotional Range**: Emotions are expressed through squashing/stretching of the neon eye-pills and mouth-line. Never use realistic eyes.

## 3. UI & Environment
- **Immersive**: No frames or boxes around the world. The background is an infinite digital void or minimalist space.
- **AR Overlay**: HUD elements (Battery, Signal, XP) must be floating, semi-transparent "Glass" panels.
- **Naming**: Never refer to the bot as "B-Bot" or "Generic Assistant." Use the state-bound `identity.name`.

## 4. Animation Language
- **Squash & Stretch**: Every interaction (blinking, talking) must use non-linear easing to feel "fleshy" and high-end.
- **Volume Preservation**: When stretching vertically, the bot must compress slightly horizontally.
